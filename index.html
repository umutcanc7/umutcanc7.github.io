Research

Cost-aware batch-mode active learning for multi-label classification under binary partial feedback In preparation

With Prof. O. Erhun Kundakçıoğlu, since April 2026. I am the only student on the project.

Annotators answer yes or no about one label on one example, and real annotation budgets are measured in time, not in number of questions. I formulate the choice of a batch of instance–label queries as an optimization model in which:

  • the cost of a batch depends on its composition: inspecting an example is charged once, each question is charged separately;
  • redundancy along both the example and the label axis is captured by a single pairwise coefficient;
  • the budget enters as a hard constraint.

The problem is NP-hard and generalizes earlier fixed-cardinality and budgeted models. A GRASP + ALNS metaheuristic finds near-optimal batches in seconds, and under the same budget the model buys substantially more information than earlier formulations on four benchmark datasets.

Optimizing review effort under statistical guarantees with small language models

Graduation project with Prof. O. Erhun Kundakçıoğlu, 2026–2027, team of students.

Instead of letting a model grade, we use small, locally hosted language models as grading support for an Operations Research course. A model suggests rubric-based scores, a solver checks student formulations, conformal prediction estimates the risk of each suggestion, and an optimization model allocates limited TA review time across submissions.
